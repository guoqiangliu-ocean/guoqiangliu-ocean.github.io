Research Focus
My central contribution is the Stochastic Vortex Force (SVF) framework, which resolves the ~60-year Snodgrass swell paradox by deriving irreversible swell dissipation from first principles with zero fitted parameters. The theory bridges Mori-Zwanzig projection, Green-Kubo relations, and Kolmogorov turbulence scaling to yield a universal dissipation rate μ(ω) ∝ εeff2/3 ω8/3.
